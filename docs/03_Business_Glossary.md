# Business Glossary

| Business Term | Definition |
|---------------|------------|
| Customer | An individual or organization that uses the bank's financial services. |
| Branch | A physical banking location where customer accounts are managed. |
| Account | A financial account (Savings or Current) owned by a customer. |
| Transaction | A financial activity such as deposit, withdrawal, or transfer performed on an account. |
| Loan | A financial product where the bank lends money to a customer with agreed repayment terms. |
| Credit Card | A payment card issued by the bank that allows customers to borrow funds up to a credit limit. |
| Employee | A bank staff member responsible for banking operations. |
| Account Balance | The current amount of money available in an account. |
| IFSC Code | A unique code identifying a bank branch for electronic fund transfers. |
| EMI | Equated Monthly Installment paid by a customer to repay a loan. |
| Credit Limit | The maximum amount a customer can spend using a credit card. |
| Home Branch | The primary branch where a customer's account is registered. |
| Active Account | An account that is operational and available for transactions. |
| Closed Account | An account that has been permanently closed. |
| Frozen Account | An account temporarily restricted from performing transactions. |
